# Hero walking figure and quote GIF

The hero uses `public/images/hero/walking-figure.webp`, the original six-frame transparent sprite sheet. Its 720ms stride holds and blends each pose for 60ms. Travel is rounded to whole strides, with 1.8-second corner rests, mirroring on return, pause/resume, and the existing accessible speech bubble.

The reveal quote section uses the user-supplied `public/images/hero/download.gif` (380 x 300, with transparency), centered in front of the original Reveal quote button above the car scene. It plays its own animation in place without travel, mirroring, or corner rests. A narrow fade at the image edges softens character entrances and clipped effects, keeping the center at full opacity. The button keeps its original keycap styling, size, and centered position. The decorative GIF passes pointer events through to the button. Clicking the button or pressing Enter or Space hides the button, reveals the quote in its original centered position, and moves focus to the quote. The GIF remains mounted and continues playing in front. The two animations share `WalkingFigure`, with the quote selecting `variant="gif"`.

Both animations pause outside the viewport and in hidden tabs. The hero supports click/keyboard pause; the quote GIF is decorative. Activating the Reveal quote button does not pause or restart the GIF. The GIF uses `download-still.webp`, a lossless first-frame export, when paused because native GIF playback cannot be paused with CSS. Reduced motion shows static images and removes the animation controls while keeping the Reveal quote button accessible.

Validation: `node scripts/walking-figure-smoke.mjs` uses the local development server on port 3000 by default (`BASE_URL` overrides it). It checks the hero sprite, travel, corner rests, pause/resume, the speech bubble, both themes, and reduced motion at 320, 375, 768, and 1440 pixels wide.
